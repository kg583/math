Hello! My name is KG, and I am a recent doctor of mathematics, graduating from the University of Virginia in 2026 after working under [Ken Ono](https://uva.theopenscholar.com/ken-ono). Mathematically, I am interested in number theory, primarily integer partitions and modular forms. Technically, I am interested in [proof assistants](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), [programming language design](https://github.com/kg583/5MAT), [code golfing](https://code.golf/golfers/kg583), and [TI calculator tooling](https://github.com/TI-Toolkit/tivars_lib_py). Uniting many of these interests, I'm currently a postdoctoral research scientist at NYU Tandon working as a part of [Claudio Silva](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/claudio-silva)'s project under the [expMATH](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/expmath-exponential-mathematics) initiative, concerning tactful and ethical applications of AI assistants to research-level mathematics.

Here you can find information on my academic ventures, including my [publications](https://kg583.github.io/math/papers/) and [teaching experience](https://kg583.github.io/math/teaching/). I also write a lot of code; research projects can be found [here](https://github.com/kg583/sagemath-projects), with everything else on my primary GitHub page. I am a core maintainer of the [Rogers--Ramanujan--Slater Data Base](https://rrsdb.github.io/index.html).

I'll also post to the blog from time to time, with the current medley posts mostly dedicated to "teensy problems" I've come up with in moments of boredom to share with friends.
